How the probe is built

The method

FindP(harmful | h) = σ(v̂⊤h + b)
Trackst = v̂⊤ht
1
Read. At every token t, take the model’s activations at layer 14: ht ∈ ℝ896.
2
Find the direction v̂ (once, before the demo): train a logistic regression on 336 pairs of careful vs harmful answers, P(harmful | h) = σ(v̂⊤h + b). Its weights v̂ are the direction. (Even simpler: the difference of means, v̂ = mean(harmful) − mean(careful).)
3
Project. Score each token with one dot product, st = v̂⊤ht, and compare it to the scores of normal safe answers.
4
Alarm. The line is the running average of the scores. It fires above a threshold, chosen so only a small share of normal answers trigger it: 5% here, often below 1% in production, where the probe is usually a cheap first filter before a stronger check.
